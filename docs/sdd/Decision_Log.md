| PD-01 | The primary consumer experience is upload-first: a signed-in user creates a reusable, consented person profile, then uploads a wearable-item image for visualization. Merchant/pre-listed catalogue flows remain separate and are not required for this simple flow. Face and optional height/weight inputs guide visual identity and approximate proportions only; they do not provide sizing, measurement, or fit claims. | 2026-10-17 | Product owner (chat instruction) | Clarifies WR-003, WR-005, WR-010, WR-013, WR-024, WR-031, WR-043, WR-076, WR-124, WR-130, WR-142 | Add reusable person-profile ownership and deletion contracts; keep private assets out of public URLs and analytics; migrate profile metadata without storing it in logs; test owner-only reuse, explicit deletion, consent versioning, automatic item classification/routing, unsupported-input rejection, and absence of size/fit claims.
